The Sandbox Philosophy

At Dumbux, we build interactive environments. We believe the web should be a living, breathing laboratory. Whether you are piloting a quadcopter through a storm in Crazy Drone or testing the laws of kinetic energy in Crazy Impulse, you are interacting with custom-built engines that prioritize physical accuracy and responsive feedback.

We embrace the "messy" side of creativity - the trial, the error, and the "what if" moments that happen when geometry meets gravity. Our mission is to bridge the gap between complex engineering and pure, unadulterated play. We strip away the loading screens and the installers to give you raw, physics-driven chaos directly in your browser.
