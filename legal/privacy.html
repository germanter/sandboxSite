Last updated: 2025-12-22

This Privacy Policy describes how Dumbux (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates and explains our practices regarding user privacy. By accessing or using this website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

Dumbux is a browser-based platform that provides sandbox-style experiences, entertaining mini-games, and experimental simulations designed for a general and young audience. We respect user privacy and are committed to keeping this website simple, transparent, and minimal in terms of data usage.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or process personal data for the general use of the website. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal identifiers for normal website usage.
  • We do not require user registration or provide login functionality.
  • The website can be used without providing any personal information.

3. User-Submitted Content via Email

If a user chooses to contact us or submit files via email (which is entirely voluntary), we may receive the sender’s email address and the content provided.

This information is used solely for:

We do not: Sell this information, share it with third parties, or use it for marketing/tracking purposes.

5. Third-Party Advertising

Providers may use device information and non-personally identifiable data to deliver relevant ads. Dumbux has no control over how third-party advertisers collect or use data.

6. Children’s Information

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 in accordance with COPPA. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

7. Purpose of the Website

Dumbux exists for sandbox-style digital experiences, entertaining mini-games, and experimental simulations. All content is for entertainment and experimental purposes only.

8. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data for general use, there is no user database to expose or misuse. Voluntary email submissions are handled with reasonable care.
